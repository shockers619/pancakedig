# Pancake Dig — Go-To-Market Playbook

Adapted from FloorBalance's `FACEBOOK_OUTREACH.md` + `BACKLINK_STRATEGY.md`. The
mechanics are FloorBalance's (they were good); the **targeting is deliberately
different**. FloorBalance sprayed every state's Facebook groups thinly and
nationally — and stalled. Pancake Dig saturates **one metro at a time**, timed
to that metro's tryout window, then fans out. The directory is national; the
*seeding and outreach* are sequential.

---

## THE STRATEGY IN ONE LINE

Be the **complete** answer for one metro before touching the next. A directory
that's thin everywhere ranks for nothing and gets shared by no one. A directory
that has *every* club in Dallas gets shared in every Dallas volleyball group.

**Seeding order:** Dallas–Fort Worth → Houston + Austin (own Texas) → Southern
California → Chicago/Great Lakes → Florida → everything else.

---

## THE VOLLEYBALL CALENDAR — this is the clock FloorBalance never had

Club volleyball tryouts spike on a schedule. Get a metro seeded and Google-indexed
*before* its window, not during it:

- **Girls 15U–18U:** tryouts ~**late July**
- **Girls 11U–14U:** tryouts ~**mid-October**
- Preseason training Aug–Nov · tournament season Dec–May · USAV Junior Nationals late June/July
- **Varies by USAV region — always confirm the target metro's regional dates.**

**Right now (August):** the 11U–14U window (mid-Oct) is the next catchable spike.
SEO pages need weeks to index, so a metro seeded in August/early September is
positioned for that October search surge.

## CHANNEL 1 — FACEBOOK GROUPS (fastest traffic)

### How not to get banned (same discipline as FloorBalance)
1. Join and engage for a day or two before posting — like/comment first, no drive-bys.
2. Never paste identical text into multiple groups the same day — rotate and tweak. FB's spam filter flags repeated text.
3. One group per ~30–60 min; 3–4 groups/day max at first. Slow is safe.
4. Link is just `pancakedig.com` — send people to the homepage to search their own area.
5. End every post with a question — posts with comments get shown to more people. Reply to every comment within the hour.
6. Read group rules. If "no promotion," use the soft/value-first variant or ask the admin — many say yes to a genuinely free local resource.

### How to find the groups (search these on Facebook)
- `[Metro] Club Volleyball` · `[Metro] Junior Volleyball` · `[State] Volleyball Parents`
- `[USAV Region] Volleyball` (e.g. "North Texas Volleyball") · `[Metro] Volleyball Recruiting`
- `Juniors Volleyball [State]` · `[Metro] Girls Volleyball`

### MASTER POST (swap metro + clubs + link)
> Hey [METRO] volleyball families 👋
>
> I got tired of bouncing between JVA, USAV, and AAU sites — plus a dozen Facebook groups — every tryout season just to find clubs, so I built a free directory that pulls them into one place: **Pancake Dig** (pancakedig.com). Search clubs, tryouts, training, venues, and events by area, division, and level — all three governing bodies in one search.
>
> [METRO] already has **[N]+** clubs on there, including [CLUB A], [CLUB B], and [CLUB C]. 100% free to search and free to list.
>
> If you run a club (or know one that should be on there), it's about 2 minutes to add: pancakedig.com
>
> Would genuinely love feedback — what would make this more useful for your family?

### SOFT / VALUE-FIRST VARIANT (for "no promotion" groups — great as a *comment* on "anyone know of tryouts?")
> Not sure if this helps, but if anyone's hunting for club tryouts this season, there's a free directory that pulls [METRO] clubs, tryouts, and training into one search across JVA/USAV/AAU — pancakedig.com. No cost. Saves the annual scavenger hunt across a dozen group feeds 😅

## CHANNEL 2 — CLUB DIRECT OUTREACH (the flywheel)

Clubs want to fill rosters in a short window — a free listing that gets them found
is a direct win *right when they care most*. And when a club claims its listing,
it shares the link and brings its parents. This is the highest-leverage channel.

### DM / email to a club already listed
> Hi Coach — I run Pancake Dig (pancakedig.com), a free directory for grassroots volleyball, and [CLUB] is already listed for the [METRO] area. Wanted to offer you the chance to **claim it** (free) so you control the info, get a verified badge, and it links back to your site. Takes ~2 minutes. Families are searching for clubs in [AREA] right now ahead of tryouts and I'd love [CLUB] to stand out. Want the claim link?

### Finding club contacts
Google `[metro] club volleyball`, the **JVA club directory**, the **USAV region**
club finder, **AAU volleyball** club listings, and club Instagram accounts. Verify
every detail against the club's **own** site (hard rule — never guess).

## CHANNEL 3 — BACKLINKS & AUTHORITY (compounding, long game)

Every backlink lifts every page's ranking. Priority tiers:

**Tier 1 — volleyball directories / governing bodies**
- USA Volleyball region sites, JVA (jvavolleyball.org), AAU Volleyball — ask to be listed as a directory resource
- **AES / Advanced Event Systems** and **SportWrench** — where tournament schedules live; partnership/backlink targets
- MaxPreps (volleyball), SportsEngine

**Tier 2 — recruiting / media (older-player parents)**
- PrepDig / PrepVolleyball, AVCA (avca.org), VolleyballMag, Off the Block (boys)
- NCSA (volleyball), SportsRecruits, On3 — resource-partner outreach

**Tier 3 — Reddit (be genuine, not spammy)**
- r/volleyball, r/womensvolleyball, state/metro subreddits (r/texas, r/dfw, etc.)

**Tier 4 — local & regional media**
- Local paper sports desks, community sports pages: "Local founder builds free national volleyball directory."

---

## PRIORITY ORDER (per metro, repeat)

1. **Seed it complete** — every club/venue/training in the metro, verified against each org's own site.
2. **Get it indexed** — sitemaps + landing pages live, ideally weeks before the metro's tryout window.
3. **Facebook** — the metro's parent/club groups (master + soft variants).
4. **Club outreach** — DM listed clubs to claim (the flywheel).
5. **Backlinks** — Tier 1–2 for that region.
6. Move to the next metro. Keep prior metros warm with periodic value posts.

---

## TRACKING
- **Google Search Console** — indexing + who links to you.
- **Google Analytics → Realtime** while posting to Facebook — watch visitors arrive live; Acquisition to see which groups convert. Double down on those.

---

## THE RULE
Complete-in-one-place beats sparse-everywhere. Saturate a metro, ride its tryout
calendar, let the claim-and-share flywheel spread it locally — then repeat. That's
the one thing FloorBalance's national-spray plan never did.
